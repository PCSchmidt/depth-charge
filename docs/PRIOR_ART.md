# Depth Charge - Prior Art and Differentiation

**Status:** Draft v1. Limited web survey on 2026-10-30; listing text only. Needs a deeper pass before any novelty claim.

## 1. Existing products found
| Product | Notes |
|---|---|
| [FREEDIVER: Triton Down](https://store.steampowered.com/app/995230) (VR) | Story-driven survival adventure with oxygen management and flooded-ship navigation; not competitive ([Road to VR](https://roadtovr.com/?p=168798)) |
| Freediving Hunter ([IGF page](https://igf.com/freediving-hunter)) | Spearfishing game with a lung-capacity system and a blackout lockout |
| [Deep Breath prototype](https://hunterproduction.itch.io/deepbreath-prototype) | Dynamic oxygen/stamina drain prototype |
| [Apnea](https://ludum-dare.foon.uk/games/57/413193) | Game-jam entry where the player holds their real breath |

The survey did not surface a competitive, discipline-based freediving simulation with ghost/ladder structure. Absence from a brief search is not proof of absence; verify.

## 2. Takeaways
- Oxygen-limited underwater mechanics are common in survival and exploration games; **physiology-driven competitive disciplines with replays and ratings** appear less covered.
- The genre's audience size is unknown; this title is best treated as a technical-art and simulation showcase.

## 3. Intended differentiation (to be validated)
1. Discipline-based competition (CWT, FIM, CNF, DYN, STA, reef race, cave swim-through) with an AIDA-style surface protocol as a game rule.
2. A coupled physiology and buoyancy model validated against analytic checks and plausible ranges.
3. Physically motivated underwater optics (wavelength attenuation, caustics, Snell's window, silt).
4. Deterministic replay-verified competition and a safe-RL pacing policy compared to an analytic optimum.

## 4. Open research tasks
- Survey freediving and underwater games including VR titles.
- Review published breath-hold physiology data and competition rules to cite parameter ranges.
- Review published optical water-type data for the attenuation model.
- Consider safety review of messaging with a qualified freediving instructor if the game is ever distributed widely.
