Depth Charge

An entertainment simulation. It is not training and not medical guidance. Never practise breath-hold diving alone or without training and supervision.

Start here

A fixed first dive with step-by-step hints and a ghost diver to race.
What is this game?
Constant Weight: swim down a line on one breath, grab a tag at the depth you announced, turn, swim back, and pass the surface protocol. Your body is the resource: oxygen runs out (you cannot see it), the urge to breathe rises (you can feel it), your ears need equalizing as pressure builds, and your weighting decides when you start to fall by yourself. A blackout is a failure, not a goal. The translucent diver is a scripted ghost playing the same dive.
